# Code of Conduct & Contribution Policy


## 1. Contribution Guidelines (Allowed & Encouraged)

**Proposing changes to this official repository is expressly permitted and welcomed:**

- **Pull Requests**: You are encouraged to propose bug fixes, performance improvements, documentation updates, and new feature ideas directly to this repository (`https://github.com/Krzyzyk33/CMDAI-CODE`).
- **Issues & Discussions**: Opening issues for bug reports, questions, or architectural feedback is actively welcomed.
- **Code Standards**: Contributions should align with the project's coding style, maintain high reliability, and preserve clean architecture.
- All contributions submitted and merged into this repository are incorporated under the terms of the project's license.

---

## 2. Strict Restriction on Derivative Works

**No derivative works, forks, or secondary implementations may be created or distributed without prior written consent:**

- **Written Permission Required**: Creating any derivative work, standalone project, commercial product, or secondary distribution based on or derived from this codebase is **strictly prohibited without explicit prior written authorization from the author and copyright holder**.
- **No Unauthorized Distribution**: You may not repackage, relicense, redistribute, or publish modifications as an independent project or service without such written consent.
- **Upstream-First Contribution**: If you wish to improve the software or adapt features, you are invited to submit your proposed improvements directly as contributions (Pull Requests) to this upstream repository.

For written authorization inquiries, please contact the repository owner directly.

---

## 3. Community Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## 5. Attribution & Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and any official communication channels associated with CMDAI CODE.
