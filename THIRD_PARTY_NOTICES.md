## Bundled assets

### `editor/icons/font/cmdai-icons.ttf` — our own work

This font is **generated by this project**, from geometric primitives
drawn with `fontTools` (`moveTo` / `lineTo` / `closePath`) in
`editor/icons/build_font.py`. No glyph outlines were copied from any
existing font. It is therefore covered by our own licence and carries
no third-party font licence.

The editor can also *use* glyphs from an installed Nerd Font when one is
present (`nerd` icon mode). No Nerd Font data is embedded, bundled, or
redistributed here — those glyphs are rendered locally by a font the
user has already installed.

### Logo

The logo is ASCII art stored in `editor/ui/logo_variants.py` and is our
own work. It was produced with the `oh-my-logo` tool
(`npx oh-my-logo "CMDAI EDITOR" --filled --block-font simple`) and the
result was pasted into the source, so the tool is **not** invoked at
runtime. **The licence of `oh-my-logo` itself has not been verified**
and is therefore not asserted here — this is a known open item.
